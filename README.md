## About

### Description and Purpose
FitForge is a personalized fitness platform that generates professional workout plans tailored to each user’s fitness level, weight, height, and goals. The platform helps users find safe and effective workouts, whether they’re beginners looking to start their fitness journey or advanced athletes trying to optimize performance. Users can create accounts, input basic details, and instantly receive structured training plans crafted to match their profile.

The app aims to make personalized fitness more accessible and data-driven, eliminating the guesswork often involved in building a consistent workout routine.

### Inspiration
The inspiration for FitForge came from noticing that many people struggle to find workout plans that truly suit their body type, schedule, and current fitness level. Most generic plans overlook personal data like height, weight, or goals, leading to inconsistent results. FitForge bridges that gap by using structured inputs and logic-based matching to deliver smarter, safer, and more effective training recommendations.

## Features

### Personalized Workout Plan Generator
Generates customized workout plans based on user data such as height, weight, fitness level, and goals.

### Progress Tracking Dashboard
Allows users to log completed workouts, track improvements, and view performance graphs over time.

### Goal-Based Filters
Users can select from goals such as muscle gain, fat loss, endurance, or flexibility to refine their workout plan.

### Category-Based Workouts
Displays categorized plans (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced) for quick access and comparison.

### Profile Management
Users can update personal data (height, weight, goals) and regenerate optimized workout plans accordingly.

### Community Forum *(Custom Feature)*
A space for users to discuss tips, share progress, and support one another.

### AI-Powered Plan Recommendations *(Custom Feature)*
Uses AI logic or preset algorithms to suggest modifications for future workout plans based on performance trends.
